What helps you move forward?

A free self-reflection from Point of View™. Explore how you think, connect, lead yourself and use technology, and leave with a clearer picture of your habits and one small action to try.

Inspired by McKinsey & Company's published research on the skills people need for the future of work, the "Distinct Elements of Talent" (DELTAs). POV is not affiliated with or endorsed by McKinsey.

This is not a test. There are no right or wrong answers — only honest ones. Take your time, reflect on each statement, and choose what feels most true for you right now.
